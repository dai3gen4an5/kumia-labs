# Electric Toothbrush Guide research record

Checked: 2026-09-14

## Selection decisions

- Oral-B Pro 1000 with CrossAction heads: a current low-cost handle using the standard Oral-B rechargeable head family. Oral-B iO heads are a separate fitting.
- Philips Sonicare 4100 HX3681/23 with C2 Plaque Control: this exact current US model documents the C2 bundle and up-to-14-day battery. Other 4100 variants have different included heads and battery claims, so the article names the model.
- Oral-B Pro 1000 Twin Pack: current US product with two handles, two CrossAction heads, and two chargers.
- Philips Sonicare 9900 Prestige HX9990/11 with A3 and charging case: chosen specifically because the included USB travel case charges the handle.
- Oral-B iO Series 2 is omitted. It is not needed for any of the four ownership cases, so the article makes no claim about the current support notice.

## Cost calculations

- CrossAction: $39.94 / 4 heads = $9.985 each. Four per year = $39.94 per person.
- CrossAction for two people: 8 heads per year = two 4-packs = $79.88 per household.
- Sonicare C2: $31.99 / 3 heads = $10.6633 each. Four per year = $42.65, rounded to cents.
- Sonicare A3: $32.99 / 2 heads = $16.495 each. Four per year = $65.98.
- Method uses each manufacturer's roughly three-month replacement guidance. Retail prices are dated snapshots and may change.

## Time-sensitive items

- Prices, sale status, stock, colors, and retailer bundle contents should be checked again when the article is materially updated.
- Philips uses the 4100 name across multiple model numbers. The included head and listed battery life must be verified against the exact SKU.
- Oral-B's own Pro 1000 pages are inconsistent about the number of modes. The article uses the current Target listing for the one-mode statement and avoids making mode count a recommendation criterion.
