## Claims intentionally excluded or qualified

- Suction Pa was recorded only as manufacturer data and not used to rank pet-hair pickup.
- Hair-removal, zero-tangle, stain-removal, bacteria, runtime, coverage, and days-between-emptying figures were not presented as universal outcomes.
- Threshold clearance depends on threshold shape, spacing, and test conditions; owners are told to measure.
- Obstacle recognition does not guarantee avoidance of cables, socks, pet waste, reflective objects, or every small item.
- Annual ownership cost was not calculated because live consumable prices, usage, solution rules, and replacement intervals are not comparable enough across all 12 systems.
- “Maintenance-free” manufacturer language was not repeated. Water tanks, bags, filters, brushes, sensors, rollers/pads, and dock wash areas remain owner tasks.

## Editorial selection rule

The four scenarios are organized by the household cleaning problem, not price tier or brand rank. Products were accepted only when a current official US page supported the system feature that solves that scenario.
