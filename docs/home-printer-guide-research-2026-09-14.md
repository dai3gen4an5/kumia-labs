# Home printer guide research record

Checked: 2026-09-14. Market: United States. Primary sources were current manufacturer product and support pages.

## Published product set

1. Mostly black documents: Brother HL-L2460DW, DCP-L2647DW, MFC-L2820DW.
2. Occasional color: Brother MFC-J1360DW, Epson Expression Home XP-5200, HP Envy 6155.
3. Family printing: Epson EcoTank ET-2980, Brother MFC-T580DW, Canon PIXMA MegaTank G3270.
4. Home office: Epson EcoTank ET-3950, Brother MFC-T780DW, HP OfficeJet Pro 9125.
5. Photos and creative projects: Epson EcoTank Photo ET-8500, ET-8550, Canon imagePROGRAF PRO-310.

The product links embedded in the article are the source list. Key selection facts were cross-checked against each maker's current US specifications. Product prices were treated only as dated snapshots and were not used to rank the machines.

## Setup 01 decision

Brother monochrome laser is the preferred starting family for a household printing mostly black documents. All three choices have automatic duplex printing, a 250-sheet tray, and the TN830/TN830XL toner with a separate DR830 drum. The useful choice is print-only versus flatbed/ADF versus fax. This is an editorial default for the use case, not a claim that Brother is the only viable monochrome-laser maker.

## Yield and running-cost notes

- Canon lists up to 6,000 black or 7,700 color pages for a full G3270 bottle set in default mode.
- Brother lists up to 7,125 black and 4,400 color starter pages for MFC-T580DW under its stated test method.
- Brother rates DR830 at approximately 15,000 pages.
- Epson advertises about four cents of ink per 4-by-6 photo for ET-8500 under its stated test conditions. Media is additional.
- A universal cost-per-page table was rejected. Coverage, cleaning, photo media, subscription terms, and non-comparable manufacturer test methods make false precision likely.

## Rejected or replaced products

- Canon MegaTank PIXMA G4280: Canon's product page says it is discontinued and no longer available. Replaced with current G3270.
- Canon MegaTank PIXMA G3290: availability signals conflicted between Canon catalog and product pages during research. It was not used because the guide needs a clearly current recommendation.
- Canon PIXMA G620: not used because its current status and role overlap were less clear than the verified ET-8500/ET-8550 photo choices.
- Canon TS8820, TS7720, and TR4720: not used because the requested candidate set already supplied clearer current low-cost configurations, and older model status required more caveats.
- Canon MAXIFY GX2020: current and credible, but its ADF and 250-sheet office configuration fits Setup 04 more than the flatbed-focused family group. The final set already had three meaningfully different Home Office choices.

## Editorial limits

No hands-on test claim is made. Exact prices, stock, and supply costs can change. Readers are directed to current manufacturer pages and told where ADF, duplex, fax, and supply-system differences affect ownership.
