# DeWalt 20V MAX battery × Bauer 20V tools — research record

Checked: 2026-09-19. Market: US (dewalt.com, harborfreight.com). No hands-on testing was done by Kumia Labs. Prices were not recorded or used.

## Verdict basis

Direct fit: **NO MATCH**. Neither manufacturer lists the other's batteries as compatible; third-party sources report the packs and tool interfaces differ physically. An unofficial third-party adapter exists but is not supported by either brand.

## Intentionally excluded or qualified

- No "safe"/"unsafe" adapter verdict. Article states adapters are unsupported by both brands and that risk is the owner's; seller warnings quoted as seller warnings.
- "20V MAX" vs "18V nominal" stated only for DeWalt (manufacturer wording).
- Physical-difference details are attributed to secondary sources, not to DeWalt or Harbor Freight.

## Remaining uncertainty

- No manufacturer statement explicitly says "DeWalt and Bauer are incompatible"; verdict rests on absence of compatibility claims plus consistent secondary reports.
- Adapter behavior across specific tools (brushless, high-draw, chargers) is unverified.
- Bauer product line/naming (Hypermax, item numbers) may change; check live listing.
